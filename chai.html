How to drink it

Make it hot, iced, or creamy.

You do not need a complicated recipe. Brew it strong, then make it your way.

Classic hot chai

Steep one tea bag in hot water. Add milk and sweetener if you like it classic.

Chai latte

Brew it extra strong, add steamed milk, and make it creamy.

Iced chai

Steep, chill, pour over ice, and add milk for a smooth cold drink.
