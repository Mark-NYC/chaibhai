Chai Bhai Brewing Guide

Two simple ways to copy the cup you had in Queens.

8 min quick version
15 min street style
2 cups made at home

The recipe

Jim's homemade masala chai.

This is built for a normal kitchen. Nothing fancy. Just tea, milk, spice, heat, and patience.

Masala Chai Base

Enough for about 2 cups. Adjust sugar and spice depending on how strong you like it.

Tea base

  • 1 heaping tbsp Assam CTC black tea
  • 1 to 2 tsp sugar, optional

Spices

  • 2 to 3 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 peppercorns
  • Small pinch ginger powder or fresh ginger

Liquid

  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup whole milk or creamy oat milk

Pro tip: Toast the spices for 20 to 30 seconds before adding water. It wakes up the aroma fast.

Quick Chai Break

Best when you want the cup without making a whole ritual out of it.

  1. 1
    Bring water, tea, sugar, and spices to a low boil for 2 to 3 minutes.
  2. 2
    Add milk and return to a gentle boil.
  3. 3
    Simmer 3 to 5 minutes until the color deepens.
  4. 4
    Strain into cups and drink it hot.

Street Chai Break

Best when you want it stronger, creamier, and closer to what you tasted with Jim.

  1. 1
    Boil water, tea, sugar, and spices for 4 to 5 minutes.
  2. 2
    Add milk and let it rise. Lower heat before it spills.
  3. 3
    Repeat the rise 2 or 3 times to thicken the flavor.
  4. 4
    Simmer 2 more minutes, strain, and serve.
