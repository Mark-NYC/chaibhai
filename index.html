Why this NYC food tour

Most NYC visitors never make it past Manhattan. That is the mistake.

Jackson Heights is one of the best food neighborhoods in New York City. In just a few blocks, you can find Tibetan momos, Indian sweets, Colombian bakeries, Nepali snacks, Mexican street food, Bangladeshi flavors, and more.

This Queens food tour helps you experience the city the way locals actually do: walking, eating, talking, and discovering places you probably would never find on your own.
